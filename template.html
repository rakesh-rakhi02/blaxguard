📚 Scam Awareness Guide

Master the 8 most common scam tactics targeting Indian citizens and test your knowledge with the interactive quiz.

🪪
1. Fake KYC Suspension Scam
KYC Trap
🔧 How it works

Fraudsters send SMS/WhatsApp saying your bank account, SIM card, or PAN link is suspended. They demand clicking an unofficial link or calling an unknown number to "complete KYC".

⚠️ Warning signs

Threatens account block within 24 hours; links ending in .xyz/.top/.live; requests NetBanking password or Aadhaar OTP on web forms.

🛡️ What to do

Never click links in KYC SMS. Visit your home bank branch or use the official mobile app. Legitimate banks never suspend accounts via SMS links.

🔢
2. OTP & Credential Theft
Credential Theft
🔧 How it works

Caller claims to be from your bank, courier delivery, or electricity board. They say an OTP was sent to confirm delivery, refund money, or cancel a penalty.

⚠️ Warning signs

Caller demands the 4 or 6-digit number on your SMS; extreme rush ("Tell me fast or session expires"); claims OTP is to "receive" money.

🛡️ What to do

Never share OTP with anyone, even if they claim to be police or bank manager. Read the SMS text carefully: banks explicitly write "Do NOT share with anyone".

📱
3. Predatory Fake Loan Apps
Extortion APK
🔧 How it works

Instant loan apps downloaded via APK links or social ads offer 5-minute loans without CIBIL. Upon installation, they secretly upload your entire contact book and photo gallery.

⚠️ Warning signs

Asks for Contacts and Photo permissions; deducts 40% upfront fee; threatens to morph photos and harass family after 7 days.

🛡️ What to do

Borrow only from RBI-registered banks and NBFCs. Never install APK files outside Google Play. Never grant Contacts or Photo permissions to lending apps.

👮
4. Digital Arrest & Police Impersonation
Coercion Fraud
🔧 How it works

Scammers call on WhatsApp video wearing fake police/CBI uniforms with official logos. They claim a parcel with illegal drugs or money laundering has your Aadhaar name.

⚠️ Warning signs

Threatens immediate arrest; conducts "court hearing" on Skype/video; orders you to stay on video call alone and keep secret from family.

🛡️ What to do

Hang up immediately. Indian Police and CBI NEVER conduct arrests, investigations, or bail proceedings over video calls. Call 1930 immediately.

🎁
5. Lottery, Prize & Parcel Scam
Advance Fee
🔧 How it works

Message claims you won ₹25 Lakh in KBC lottery, a luxury car in a lucky draw, or an iPhone. To claim, you must pay "customs duty", "GST", or "processing charges" in advance.

⚠️ Warning signs

You never bought a ticket or entered any contest; demands advance deposit to unlock money; sends fake letters with forged government stamps.

🛡️ What to do

Remember: You can NEVER win a lottery or contest you didn't enter! Genuine prizes never demand advance money. Block the sender and report.

💸
6. UPI "Collect Request" Trap
UPI Reversal
🔧 How it works

A scammer pretending to buy your item on OLX or send a refund says: "I am sending money, please scan this QR code or accept the request and enter your UPI PIN".

⚠️ Warning signs

Asking you to enter UPI PIN to "receive" money; QR code sent on WhatsApp with instructions to scan and enter PIN.

🛡️ What to do

Golden Rule: UPI PIN is ONLY needed to SEND money, NEVER to receive money! If anyone tells you to enter your PIN to receive money, decline the request immediately.

🎧
7. Fake Customer Care & Remote Apps
Remote Access
🔧 How it works

Fraudsters post fake mobile numbers on Google Maps for banks, airlines, or delivery services. When called, they ask you to install "AnyDesk", "TeamViewer", or "QuickSupport".

⚠️ Warning signs

10-digit mobile number listed instead of 1800 toll-free; asking you to install screen-sharing apps; asking to send ₹10 to "verify" account.

🛡️ What to do

Always get helpline numbers directly from the official bank passbook, official app, or bank portal. Never install remote screen-sharing apps on a caller's request.

💼
8. Work-From-Home & Task Scams
Prepaid Task
🔧 How it works

WhatsApp/Telegram message offering ₹3,000–₹5,000/day for liking YouTube videos or rating hotels. Small initial payout builds trust, followed by demands to invest in "prepaid crypto tasks".

⚠️ Warning signs

Work from home with unrealistically high pay; Telegram group with fake member earnings; asking you to deposit money to "unlock" your earnings.

🛡️ What to do

Legitimate employers NEVER ask you to pay money to get a job or task payout. Cease communication immediately as soon as a deposit is requested.

Crucial Reminder: Use Only Bank's Official Number
Never search for your bank's customer care on Google Search or Google Maps — scammers post fake mobile numbers there. Look only on the back of your physical ATM/Debit card or inside your printed passbook.

⏱️ First 60 Minutes After Losing Money (The Golden Hour)

Banks and 1930 nodal officers can trigger an emergency freeze on beneficiary accounts if reported immediately. Follow these 5 steps in order:

  • 1
    Dial 1930 Immediately: Provide your account number, bank name, transaction time, and amount. The 1930 operator triggers a CFCFRMS ticket to freeze the scammer's bank account before they withdraw cash.
  • 2
    Freeze Card & UPI in Bank App: Open your official mobile banking app right now. Go to Card Controls and lock your Debit card. Turn off online and international transactions.
  • 3
    Save 12-Digit UTR & Transaction Details: Take screenshots of debit SMS and UPI receipts. Note down the 12-digit UTR/UPI reference number, scammer's UPI ID/number, and exact timestamp.
  • 4
    Lodge Complaint on cybercrime.gov.in: Visit the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal to log an official police complaint and receive an acknowledgement number for your bank dispute.
  • 5
    Notify Bank in Writing within 3 Days: Submit a formal written dispute letter to your home bank branch manager with the 1930 complaint number. Under RBI guidelines, notifying the bank promptly protects your entitlement to refund compensation.
🔒

How to Freeze Your Card & UPI in Bank App (30 Seconds)

Even if a scammer stole your card number or PIN, they cannot deduct another rupee once you switch off card usage in your official bank app:

1️⃣ Open your genuine bank app (e.g. YONO SBI, HDFC MobileBanking, iMobile, Baroda M-Connect).
2️⃣ Go to "Cards" → "Manage Debit Card" → Toggle "Temporary Lock / Block Card".
3️⃣ Turn OFF "Domestic Online / E-Commerce" and "ATM Cash Withdrawal" toggles.
4️⃣ Under "UPI Settings" → Select "Deregister UPI Profile" or reduce your daily limit to ₹1.
